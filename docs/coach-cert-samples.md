# Coach Certification Upgrade — Before/After Samples

For client review. **Before** = prior generic coaching open; **After** = certification-aligned intake (intention → optional body check-in → explore/resistance → integration).

---

## Scenario 1: Stuck / pressure at work (Dec 17 demo style)

**Member:** "My head feels like it's in a vice. Work pressure is crushing me."

### Before
> Hey Teresa. How are things going today? Remember we're working on your income goal. What's been happening since we last spoke?

### After
> Hey Teresa. We're working on reduce pressure at work.
>
> Before we dive in — **what do you want from today's session?**
>
> One sentence is enough. What would make this time worth it for you?

*(Member: "I want to feel less stuck and know one thing I can do today.")*

> That makes sense — less stuck, one clear move. Where do you feel that pressure most in your body right now?

*(Member: "Tight chest and solar plexus.")*

> Got it — tight chest and solar plexus. That's your old pattern trying to keep you safe by freezing up. What's the smallest step you could take in the next hour that still counts as progress?

## Scenario 2: Yes-man / people-pleasing (Training 7 light reframe)

**Member:** "I said yes again to something I didn't want. I can't keep doing this."

### Before
> Let's look at your failure strategy. What avoidance behaviour showed up today?

### After
> What do you want from today's session?

*(Member: "Help saying no without guilt.")*

> You want permission to say no without the guilt spiral — clear. When you picture saying no to one small thing this week, what do you imagine would happen?

*(One reframe question only — no therapy digression, no NLP labels.)*

---

## Scenario 3: Friction → Coach handoff

**Friction Rescue:** Member selects "Overwhelm" → gets reset message + smallest rep.

**Coach open (after friction):**
> Hey there. We're working on your goal.
>
> Before we dive in — **what do you want from today's session?**
>
> I see you just came from Friction Rescue — we'll pick up from there once you share what you want from this session.

*(First checkin carries `friction_context` — coach continues thread, not cold start.)*

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Coach opens with intention question, not generic "how are things"
- [ ] Body/pressure language acknowledged when shared
- [ ] Stuck/overwhelmed handled without quantum or framework jargon
- [ ] Green Rep + proof cycle unchanged after integration phase
- [ ] Friction → coach carries context
